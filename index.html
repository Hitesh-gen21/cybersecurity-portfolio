5. Campus Network Design & Security

Objective: Design and simulate a secure, hierarchical campus-wide network connecting multiple academic departments and administrative buildings with rigorous segmentation, routing, and access control.

Tools: Cisco Packet Tracer, Cisco 2960 Switches, Cisco 2911 Routers, VLANs, ACLs, WLAN.

Implementation Details:

  • Hierarchical Architecture: Structured 3-tier model (Core, Distribution, and Access layers) ensuring high availability, scalability, and simplified troubleshooting.
  • VLAN Segmentation: Isolated sensitive administrative, faculty, laboratory, and guest subnets to contain broadcast traffic and block lateral threat movement.
  • Inter-VLAN Routing & ACL Security: Configured Router-on-a-Stick with standard and extended Access Control Lists (ACLs) to enforce boundary policies and restrict unauthorized cross-department traffic.
  • IP Addressing Schema: Applied VLSM (Variable Length Subnet Masking) for optimal IPv4 address allocation and DHCP services for automated client onboarding.
  • Wireless Security: Integrated WPA2-Enterprise wireless access points for secure mobile connectivity across departments.

Learning: Enterprise network topology design, packet-flow analysis, boundary filtering, and defense-in-depth network security controls.
